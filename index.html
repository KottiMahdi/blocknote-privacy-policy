Privacy Policy for NoteNest

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Thank you for using NoteNest. This Privacy Policy explains what information NoteNest collects, how it is used, stored, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account or sign in, NoteNest and its authentication providers may process:

  • Your email address and Firebase user ID.
  • Your username or profile name.
  • Google account information, if you choose Google sign-in.

We use this information to authenticate your account and provide access to NoteNest.

Notes and categories

NoteNest stores the content and details you create, including:

  • Notes and note titles.
  • Category names and settings, such as category type, icon, and color.
  • Associated account identifiers and note creation timestamps.

This information is used to save and synchronize your notes and categories across your devices.

App preferences

NoteNest saves your light or dark theme preference locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate your account.
  • Save and synchronize your notes and categories.
  • Provide NoteNest's core functionality.
  • Maintain the reliability and security of the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Account and app data are stored using Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Data transmitted between your device and Firebase is protected in transit using HTTPS.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their advertising or marketing purposes. Google Firebase processes information as needed to provide authentication, storage, and related services. Google's handling of information is also subject to Google's privacy terms.

5. Data Security

We rely on reasonable safeguards and Firebase security features to help protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. No method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You may access or update your account information and delete your account from the app's settings. When you delete your account using this feature, NoteNest deletes the associated profile, categories, notes, and Firebase Authentication account. You may also contact us with questions or requests about your personal data.

7. Children's Privacy

NoteNest is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact us at mkotti72@gmail.com.
